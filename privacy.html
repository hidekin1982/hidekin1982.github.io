Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Who uses this app

BGM Uploader is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account. It is not distributed and has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel
  • youtube.readonly — to confirm which channel it is connected to and list its videos
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read the channel's watch-time statistics

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to perform the actions above for the owner. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and none is used for advertising or profiling.

Google API Services User Data Policy

BGM Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Please open an issue at https://github.com/hidekin1982/hidekin1982.github.io/issues.
