BGM Uploader

BGM Uploader is a small private tool. Its owner uses it to upload long-form ambient videos to their own YouTube channel and to read that channel's basic statistics.

It is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up, no other users, and it never accesses anyone else's Google account.
